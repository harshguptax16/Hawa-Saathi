> **"Hawa Sarthi" (हवा सारथी)** is a GenAI-powered environmental defense assistant designed to help families and urban communities navigate severe winter smog and toxic air pollution. It combines **multimodal computer vision**, **structured lifestyle generation**, **low-cost DIY engineering**, and **rigorous impact quantification** to turn invisible particulate hazards into actionable daily routines.

---

## 🌟 Core Features & Modules

### 1. 📡 Hyperlocal Smog & Inversion Radar
- **Live Diurnal Smog Cycle**: Explains *why* pollution spikes during morning inversion traps (6:00 AM – 9:30 AM) and pinpoints the safe solar ventilation windows (1:30 PM – 3:15 PM).
- **Multi-Pollutant Breakdown**: Live tracking of PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, CO, SO₂ with WHO guideline comparisons.
- **Inversion Explainer**: Real-time atmospheric boundary layer physics explaining ground-level smoke trapping.

### 2. 📅 AI-Optimized Personalized Daily Routine Planner
- **Demographic Adaptation**: Automatically modifies recommendations based on family profile (infants, elderly, asthmatics/allergies, commute mode, housing type).
- **Actionable Steps**: Morning perimeter seal, commute armor (recirculation mode in cars, certified N95 fitting), damp microfiber mopping schedules, and overnight sleep shields.
- **Sustainable Alternatives & Impact**: Direct explanations showing how carpooling saves ~4.2 kg CO₂/day and damp mopping cuts resuspended PM10 by 62%.

### 3. 🔍 Room Doctor AI (Multimodal Vision IAQ Audit)
- **Computer Vision Diagnostics**: Upload a room photo (or choose a preset) to detect window draft gaps, high-dust reservoir fabrics, and stagnant dead-air zones.
- **Indoor Clean Air Protection Score (0-100)**: Quantitative scoring of room isolation.
- **Actionable Low-Cost Fixes**: Self-adhesive weatherstrip foam, draft guards, and filter placement.

### 4. 🛠️ DIY IAQ Hack Studio: Corsi-Rosenthal Box Blueprint
- **88% Savings vs Commercial Purifiers**: Build a medical-grade 450+ CFM clean air cube for ₹2,200 – ₹2,800 ($28 - $35) vs ₹25,000+ ($300+) commercial units.
- **Interactive Assembly Guide**: 4-step checklist with aerodynamic corner shrouding and airflow direction checks.
- **Plant Bio-Filter Science**: Debunks myths by explaining what indoor plants do (absorb volatile VOCs) vs what they cannot do (filter PM2.5 dust).

### 5. ⚡ Direct Impact & Co-benefit Simulator
- **Cigarettes Avoided Counter**: Uses the peer-reviewed Berkeley Earth formula ($22\,\mu\text{g/m}^3 \approx 1\text{ cigarette/day}$) to display how many equivalent cigarettes of smoke your family spared their lungs.
- **Micrograms Averted**: Exact calculation of PM2.5 prevented from penetrating deep alveolar air sacs.
- **Community Emissions Saved**: kg of CO₂ prevented and grams of primary particulate emissions stopped.
- **Interactive Confetti Celebration**: Rewards milestones of clean-air defense.

### 6. 👥 Community Clean-Air Defense & Smog Spotter
- **Citizen Hotspot Logging**: Report illegal garbage burning, dry leaf fires, and unmitigated construction dust.
- **AI Municipal Grievance Generator**: Automatically drafts a formal, legally grounded complaint letter under the Air Act and GRAP guidelines ready for municipal portals.

### 7. 🎙️ Floating Bilingual AI Voice Copilot
- **Voice-in / Voice-out**: Real-time Web Speech recognition and bilingual Text-to-Speech (TTS) in **Hindi** and **English**.
- **Instant Answers**: Ask questions like *"Can I jog this morning?"*, *"Which mask works for kids?"*, or *"कम खर्च में एयर फिल्टर कैसे बनाएं?"*.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Node.js (v18+ or v22+)
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari)

### Installation & Run

```bash
# Clone or navigate to the workspace
cd "c:\Users\HP\Downloads\Challenge 6"

# Run development server
npm run dev

# Or build for production
npm run build
npm run preview
```

Open [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173) in your browser.
